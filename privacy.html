Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MyPDF ("we", "us") handles information when you use this website.

Your files

MyPDF processes all files locally in your web browser. Files you select are never uploaded to our servers, and we cannot see, store or share them.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider may log basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit. This information is used for security and to keep the site running.

Cookies and local storage

MyPDF does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser's local storage keeps your theme choice and the activity counters shown on the home page. This data stays on your device, and you can clear it at any time from your browser settings.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page.
