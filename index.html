Known limitations

Please check these before relying on MyPDF for important documents.

File size depends on your device

Large files are processed in your device's memory. On phones, keep files under about 100 MB or 300 pages. Bigger files may be slow or may crash the browser tab.

No password-protected PDFs

Encrypted or password-protected PDFs aren't supported. Remove the password first, using an app that has it.

Signatures are visual, not certified

Sign PDF places an image of your signature. It is not a certified digital signature (PKI / eIDAS), and some legal or government documents require one.

Bookmarks and forms may be lost

Merge and Split keep the page content, but bookmarks (the outline) are dropped and fillable form fields may stop working.

Image format support varies by browser

iPhone HEIC photos only work in Safari. In other browsers, convert them to JPG first. Animated GIFs use only the first frame.

No OCR, editing or compression (yet)

MyPDF can't recognise text in scans, edit existing text or make PDFs smaller.

Rendering differences

PDF to JPG uses a browser renderer. PDFs with unusual fonts or complex graphics may look slightly different from Adobe Acrobat.

Modern browser required

Use a recent version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. Internet Explorer isn't supported.

MOD_01 // CONVERT

JPG to PDF

Convert images into one PDF. Drag the cards to change the page order.

Limitations for this tool
  • HEIC (iPhone) photos only work in Safari. Convert them to JPG first in other browsers.
  • Animated GIFs use only the first frame.
  • Images are re-saved at 92% JPEG quality, and transparent areas become white.
  • Very large or numerous images may be slow on phones.
MOD_02 // CONVERT

PDF to JPG

Turn each page of a PDF into an image.

Limitations for this tool
  • Password-protected PDFs can't be opened.
  • "Very high" quality on long documents uses a lot of memory. Use "High" on phones.
  • Unusual fonts or complex graphics may look slightly different from Adobe Acrobat.
MOD_03 // ORGANIZE

Merge PDF

Combine PDFs in the order you want. Drag the cards to reorder them.

Limitations for this tool
  • Bookmarks (the outline) aren't kept in the merged file.
  • Fillable form fields may stop working after merging.
  • Password-protected PDFs aren't supported.
MOD_04 // ORGANIZE

Split PDF

Separate pages or page ranges into new PDF files.

Limitations for this tool
  • Previews show the first 60 pages, but splitting still covers every page.
  • Bookmarks aren't kept in the split files.
  • Splitting into many files downloads a single ZIP.

Why choose MyPDF?

MyPDF is a free online PDF toolkit that runs entirely in your web browser. Unlike many other PDF sites, MyPDF never uploads your documents to a server. Converting, merging, splitting and signing all happen on your own computer or phone, so your contracts, invoices and personal files stay private.

Frequently asked questions

Is MyPDF really free?

Yes. Every tool is free to use with no sign-up and no watermarks.

Are my files safe?

Your files never leave your device. All processing happens locally in your browser.

Is the signature legally binding?

MyPDF adds a visual signature image to your PDF. This is enough for many everyday documents. Some legal documents require a certified digital signature, so check the requirements for your document.

Is there a file size limit?

There is no fixed limit, but very large files depend on your device's memory. See Known limitations.
